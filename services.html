MOT

From£49.50

To ensure that your vehicle meets safety and environmental standards, it is essential to have it undergo an MOT test. This test examines various aspects of your vehicle to ensure it meets the required standards.

At MARS, we have five highly trained MOT testers who receive yearly training to keep up to date with the latest testing methods. We also recently installed a state-of-the-art one-man test bay to further enhance our testing capabilities. As a DVSA-approved class 4 test site (Test Site Number: 10659), we offer MOT tests starting from just £49.50.

To make things more convenient for you, we provide waiting slots that can be booked in advance. Our location near the town centre also allows you to go shopping while your test is being carried out. Additionally, we offer FREE MOT retests to ensure that any issues that arise are promptly addressed.

Air Conditioning

From£54

Whether it's the warmer months or colder months, your vehicle uses the air conditioning system more than you think. Here at MARS we have machines for both the new and old systems.

The older system uses a gas called R134a, which is the most common. From 2014/2015 a newer, more expensive gas called 1234yf is used. If you're unsure which system you have, please contact us and we will let you know. Our services start at £54.

It's recommended that systems are serviced and regassed every two years.
